# 09 — Expose the privacy-safe People view

**What to build:** Give Homeowners an understandable directory of people relevant to their Build while withholding private identity, organization, operational, performance, and lender metadata. The view should make approved contact paths discoverable without turning directory visibility into broader collaboration or Build authorization.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Enter the Homeowner portfolio and shared Collaboration Home.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The view shows each eligible person's display name, Build role, approved company or trade label, avatar, and approved contact action.
- [ ] Co-homeowners, relevant Builder participants, and authorized Backoffice contacts appear according to current Build participation.
- [ ] Contractors appear only while an active assignment and Homeowner-visible scope make them relevant to the Build.
- [ ] Personal phone numbers and email addresses remain hidden unless explicitly shared through the approved contact projection.
- [ ] Organization membership details, role internals, permissions, internal titles, availability, assignment economics, performance data, and lender-only metadata are excluded.
- [ ] Directory presence does not grant access to a post, attachment, referenced entity, Action Item, or notification.
- [ ] Removed or revoked participants disappear from current directory results without erasing their historical authored content.
- [ ] Empty, partial, pending, restricted, and unavailable states disclose no hidden participant counts or identities.
- [ ] Contact actions and collaboration deep links reauthorize the participant and current Build relationship.
- [ ] Privacy, assignment-change, revocation, contractor-scope, and multi-organization behavior are covered through projection and route tests.
