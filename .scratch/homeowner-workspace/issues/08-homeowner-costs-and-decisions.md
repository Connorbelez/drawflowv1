# 08 — Project read-only Costs & Decisions

**What to build:** Show Homeowners explicitly shared project-commercial information without exposing lender financing or creating binding v1 decisions. Homeowners can understand and discuss quotes, Cost Documents, selections, change orders, and obligations through the shared collaboration record, but cannot change authoritative commercial status.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Enable full Homeowner collaboration authorship and audiences.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The view shows only quotes, Cost Documents, selections, change orders, and Homeowner obligations explicitly shared with the current Homeowner and Build.
- [ ] Project-commercial visibility remains separate from lender-financing visibility.
- [ ] Working-capital analysis, lender policy limits, interest calculations, internal Draw state, risk, review, and approval deliberation are excluded.
- [ ] A Homeowner can open or create an entity-linked collaboration conversation about each visible commercial record.
- [ ] Comments, reactions, acknowledgements, Action Items, and freeform statements cannot accept a quote, approve a selection, approve a change order, or confirm a binding obligation.
- [ ] Every commercial record remains read-only even when the Homeowner has broad collaboration capabilities.
- [ ] Dedicated governed submission flows already authorized for the Homeowner remain separate and retain their validation and audit contracts.
- [ ] Restricted records are absent from counts, search, notifications, exports, autocomplete, and retrieval.
- [ ] Version changes preserve the exact visible historical context of linked collaboration without converting old discussion into approval of a new version.
- [ ] Tests prove read-only behavior, commercial-versus-financing separation, contextual discussion, version safety, and negative authorization cases.
