# 03 — Enable full Homeowner collaboration authorship and audiences

**What to build:** Make Homeowners full human authors in the shared Build Collaboration record. They can post freeform content, participate in discussions, react, edit, tombstone, and report through the unchanged collaboration interface, while the existing audience hierarchy keeps co-homeowners and authorized coordinating roles informed and prevents private side channels.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Enter the Homeowner portfolio and shared Collaboration Home.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Homeowner can publish a freeform post without Builder preapproval or topic-level moderation.
- [ ] A Homeowner can comment, reply, follow, unfollow where permitted, and use the shared non-binding reactions available to the role.
- [ ] Reactions, comments, and acknowledgements never count as commercial approval, Action Item completion, lender approval, or formal acceptance.
- [ ] A Homeowner can edit their own post or comment with immutable revision history and an Edited indication.
- [ ] A Homeowner can remove their own content through an audited tombstone without destroying revisions, attachments, references, or receipts.
- [ ] A Homeowner can report visible content without automatically hiding it; confirmed security threats may still be quarantined by the shared safety system.
- [ ] A Homeowner cannot moderate another participant, while authorized hierarchical moderation retains a reason, history, notification, and appeal path.
- [ ] Every co-homeowner assigned to the Build is a mandatory peer reader of Homeowner-authored collaboration.
- [ ] Authorized Builder and Backoffice/Lender participants are mandatory readers of Homeowner-authored collaboration.
- [ ] A Contractor is excluded unless explicitly included, actively assigned to the Build, and authorized for the post's complete scope.
- [ ] Homeowners cannot create direct messages, private Homeowner-to-Builder conversations, or audiences hidden from co-homeowners and mandatory readers.
- [ ] Publication, discussion, editing, tombstoning, reporting, moderation denial, and audience behavior are covered through shared collaboration tests and one authenticated Homeowner browser flow.
