# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Builder / Developer Principal**: Professional builder or developer managing ground-up construction financed through a construction loan. Needs clear milestone roadmap, low-friction evidence upload, visibility into upcoming reimbursements, and understanding of how cash on hand affects sequencing.

**Lender Admin**: Lender-side user with final authority over proposal approval, budget acceptance, milestone completion, and draw release. Needs dense control-plane workspace with clear status, risk visibility, and audit trail.

**Lender Staff / Operations Analyst**: Reviews borrower submissions, evaluates evidence, requests missing info, performs site visits. Needs kanban work queue and evidence package viewer.

All users are professionals with domain expertise. No hand-holding required.

## Product Purpose

DrawFlow is a construction lending draw-management module. It makes two coupled plans explicit and interactive: the construction plan (what work happens, in what order, with what costs) and the capital release plan (when completed work becomes eligible for reimbursement, how milestones bundle into draws, and how timing affects fees and cash flow).

The core value: eliminate spreadsheets, email threads, and PDF dumps from draw management. Replace them with a shared, auditable, optimizable workspace.

## Brand Personality

Precision power. Industrial-modern. Confident. Unapologetically bold.

Voice: direct, expert, no filler. Every word earns its place.
Tone: capable, not coddling. The interface respects the user's domain expertise.
Emotional goal: users feel in control, not overwhelmed. The tool disappears; the work shines.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboard templates (hero metrics, gradient cards, "friendly" copy)
- Construction software from 2005 (clunky forms, dense tables, bevelled buttons, system gray)
- Overly playful fintech (emoji, bright gradients, cutesy empty states)
- Category-reflex dark-blue "observability" or teal "healthcare" palettes
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text repeated endlessly
- Side-stripe borders, gradient text, glassmorphism as decoration

## Design Principles

1. **The plan is the interface**: The construction roadmap and draw schedule are not views you navigate to; they ARE the workspace. Everything else orbits around them.
2. **Show state, don't explain it**: A milestone's status should be immediately readable from its visual treatment, not from a label or tooltip.
3. **Density without clutter**: Professionals need information density. Use hierarchy, spacing, and color roles to organize, not whitespace padding.
4. **Motion is directional, not decorative**: Transitions should communicate spatial relationships (forward/back, expand/collapse), not entertain.
5. **Respect the user's time**: No "welcome to..." intros. No restated headings. No ceremony. Get them to the plan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Sufficient color contrast even for color-coded status indicators (shape or icon backup)
- Keyboard-navigable for all primary actions
