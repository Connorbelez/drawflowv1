# DrawFlow Core Product Workflow Manifest

> Canonical, source-backed workflow inventory. Generated by `scripts/core-workflow-manifest.ts`; do not edit the generated file by hand.

## Scope and conventions

This manifest models reimbursement-only DrawFlow workflows from tenant provisioning through build execution, evidence verification, draw release, receipt, notifications, and integrations. Parent workflows are end-to-end processes; persona segments are the only persona-specific views of those parents. Shared handoff IDs are defined once on the parent and referenced by both sending and receiving segments.

Identifiers use `WF-{DOMAIN}-{NNN}` for parents, `WF-{DOMAIN}-{NNN}.{PERSONA}.{NN}` for segments, `WF-{DOMAIN}-{NNN}.HO-{NN}` for handoffs, and `{workflow-or-segment}.STEP-{NN}` for steps. `SUPPORTED` means directly supported by cited documentation. `NEEDS_VALIDATION` means the source is incomplete or conflicting; the exact gap is stated and no unsupported behavior is silently asserted.

### Domain invariants applied throughout

- V1 is reimbursement-only: work is completed, evidenced, reviewed, approved, and only then released.
- Interest starts only after funds are released; request and approval do not start interest.
- Borrower Working Capital Limit and Lender Draw Policy Limit are distinct constraints and are never collapsed.
- Lender Operations may review, inspect, report, prepare, and recommend; Lender Admin/Principal Broker retains final milestone and release authority.
- Geofence failure, low confidence, or suspected spoofed location never discards evidence; it creates an explicit review/override path.
- Approved Budgets are versioned, never overwritten.
- Material decisions/overrides capture actor, role, timestamp, prior/new state, warnings, and reason where applicable.
- Every domain record and workflow action is organization-scoped; WorkOS projection tables remain webhook-owned.

## Cross-persona parent workflows

### WF-TEN-001 — Brokerage Provisioning and Principal Authority Activation

1. **Unique workflow ID:** `WF-TEN-001`
2. **Workflow name:** Brokerage Provisioning and Principal Authority Activation
3. **Persona:** Cross-persona parent workflow
4. **Functional category:** Tenancy and identity
5. **Parent workflow ID:** Not applicable — this is the parent workflow.
6. **Purpose and intended outcome:** Provision one organization-scoped brokerage with exactly one active Principal Broker before product operations begin.
7. **Preconditions:**
  - Platform Admin is authenticated with platform scope.
  - No conflicting active brokerage or Principal Broker assignment exists.
8. **Trigger:** Platform Admin elects to provision a brokerage or replace its Principal Broker.
9. **Ordered workflow steps:**
  1. `WF-TEN-001.PARENT.00.STEP-01` — Create the brokerage and WorkOS Organization.
  2. `WF-TEN-001.PARENT.00.STEP-02` — Invite the first Principal Broker.
  3. `WF-TEN-001.PARENT.00.STEP-03` — Principal Broker accepts and establishes an active membership.
  4. `WF-TEN-001.PARENT.00.STEP-04` — Activate the brokerage only after the principal is active.
  5. `WF-TEN-001.PARENT.00.STEP-05` — Record provisioning and authority assignment.
10. **Inputs and required artifacts:**
  - Brokerage identity and organization metadata.
  - Principal Broker identity/email.
  - Replacement reason when transferring principal authority.
11. **Outputs and generated artifacts:**
  - WorkOS Organization.
  - Active brokerage.
  - Single active Principal Broker assignment.
  - Audit history.
12. **System states and state transitions:**
  - brokerage: unprovisioned → pending_principal → active
  - principal invitation: pending → accepted/expired/revoked
  - replacement: prior_active → historical; new_pending → active
13. **Decisions, validations, and approval gates:**
  - A brokerage cannot activate without one active Principal Broker.
  - A brokerage cannot have two active Principal Brokers.
14. **Exceptions, rejection paths, and recovery flows:**
  - Expired/revoked invitation is reissued.
  - Duplicate principal assignment is rejected.
  - Principal transfer preserves historical authority.
15. **Permissions and role constraints:**
  - Only Platform Admin provisions brokerages or performs platform-level principal replacement.
  - Principal acceptance is limited to the invited identity.
16. **Upstream and downstream workflow dependencies:**
  - Upstream: None.
  - Downstream: `WF-TEN-002`, `WF-INT-001`
17. **Cross-persona handoffs:**
  - `WF-TEN-001.HO-01` — **Platform Admin → Lender Admin / Principal Broker.** Trigger: Principal invitation is created. Artifacts: Brokerage identity, WorkOS Organization invitation, assigned principal role, expiry metadata. Required acknowledgement/next action: Invitee accepts through WorkOS/AuthKit and obtains active organization membership.
  - `WF-TEN-001.HO-02` — **DrawFlow System → Platform Admin.** Trigger: Principal membership becomes active or the invitation fails/expires. Artifacts: Membership/role sync result, brokerage activation eligibility, failure metadata. Required acknowledgement/next action: Platform Admin verifies activation or reissues/repairs the assignment.
18. **Audit, notification, and integration events:**
  - WorkOS organization/invitation events.
  - Brokerage provisioned/activated audit events.
  - Principal assigned/replaced audit events.
19. **Terminal success and failure states:**
  - Success:
    - Brokerage is active with exactly one active Principal Broker and complete audit history.
  - Failure:
    - Brokerage remains pending/inactive; no organization-scoped product work may start.
20. **Source references:**
  - `docs/draw_flow_production_prd.md §4.1–4.2, §8.1`
  - `docs/auth-rbac-foundation.md (WorkOS organization and RBAC foundation)`
**Support status:** `SUPPORTED`

1. **Unique workflow ID:** `WF-COM-001`
