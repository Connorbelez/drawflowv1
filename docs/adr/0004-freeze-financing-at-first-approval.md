# Freeze the Financing Package at first approval

The Financing Package and every Proposed Loan become immutable at the earliest Primary Construction Lender or Back Office approval of a Build Proposal. Secondary Loan Lenders are read-only and cannot approve or trigger this cutoff. No Loan may be introduced after the cutoff or during the active Build. This prevents an approved capital plan from changing underneath lender or Back Office decisions and removes the need for planned and actual financing compositions to coexist after approval. Future financing activity uses one schedule-event lineage that links immutable scheduled events to posted Loan Transactions while retaining variance. Correcting the package requires returning to a pre-approval proposal state rather than mutating an approved or active Build.
